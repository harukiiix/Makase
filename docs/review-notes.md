## プロの画面デザイナー

**Persona:** Senior product designer from a studio that makes polished mobile-first consumer and learning apps. I played the app for the first time, in Japanese first and then in English.

**Reached:** I played Monday and Tuesday all the way through, answering wrong on purpose. I left lessons in the middle twice, once by closing the browser and once with the back arrow and the "go home" choice, and came back with 続きをする. I also tapped 最初からやり直す on a lesson that was half done. I moved the clock forward 2 days and played the 10-question review. With the AI stand-in I used the AI tutor sheet (the suggestion chips, the canned answer, the stop button) and the AI practice round. I opened the notebook, settings (and switched a setting), the how-to sheet, and この学び方について, including one 出典 sources section. Devices: iPhone 13, iPhone SE, a landscape phone (844x390), desktop 1440x900 and 1280x720, dark mode, and English. I also tested keyboard focus and reduced motion. All scripts and screenshots are in /tmp/claude-0/-workspace/00163da8-532f-502b-b437-be465354297c/scratchpad/v5/review3/pd-senior/. Text contrast passed WCAG AA on every screen I scanned, the focus ring is clear (3px blue), and reduced motion turns off the animations.

**Verdict:** The look is close to professional: the colors and fonts are consistent, the dark theme is careful, contrast and focus pass, and the pixel art and copy are clean. What still holds it back is the main moment after answering on phones. The bottom sheet covers 64% of the screen and the automatic scroll hides the learner's own marked answer; in landscape it hides every answer, and the why, real-incident and AI buttons sit out of sight. Around that are a few unguarded or unfinished edges: one-tap restart, a settings gear you can't see in dark mode, review results with no way forward, and points that don't add up.
